# Sample Call Scripts for AI Receptionist Demo

## Call 1: Law Firm - New Client Consultation Inquiry

**AI:** Thank you for calling Thompson & Associates Law Firm. This is Sarah, how may I help you today?

**Caller:** Hi, I was in a car accident last week and I need to speak with someone about my options.

**AI:** I'm sorry to hear about your accident. I'd be happy to help you schedule a consultation with one of our personal injury attorneys. Can I get your name please?

**Caller:** Yes, it's Michael Rodriguez.

**AI:** Thank you Michael. And what's the best phone number to reach you?

**Caller:** It's 555-0123.

**AI:** Perfect. Our attorneys are available for consultations Monday through Friday. Would you prefer morning or afternoon?

**Caller:** Afternoon works better for me.

**AI:** Great. I have Thursday at 2 PM or Friday at 3 PM available. Which works best for you?

**Caller:** Thursday at 2 PM sounds good.

**AI:** Excellent. I've scheduled your consultation with Attorney Thompson for this Thursday at 2 PM. You'll receive a confirmation text at 555-0123. Is there anything else I can help you with today?

**Caller:** No, that's all. Thank you.

**AI:** You're welcome Michael. We'll see you Thursday. Have a great day.

## Call 2: Medical Practice - Appointment Scheduling

**AI:** Good morning, Riverside Family Medicine. This is Emma speaking. How can I assist you?

**Caller:** Hi, I need to schedule my annual physical.

**AI:** I'd be happy to help with that. May I have your name and date of birth please?

**Caller:** Jennifer Park, May 15th, 1985.

**AI:** Thank you Ms. Park. I see you're an existing patient. Are you experiencing any specific health concerns, or is this just your routine checkup?

**Caller:** Just routine, but I'd also like to discuss some bloodwork results.

**AI:** Understood. For a physical with lab review, I recommend scheduling a 30-minute appointment with Dr. Martinez. We have availability next Tuesday at 9 AM or Wednesday at 1:30 PM. Which works better for you?

**Caller:** Wednesday at 1:30 works perfectly.

**AI:** Perfect. I've scheduled you for Wednesday, April 24th at 1:30 PM with Dr. Martinez. Please arrive 10 minutes early to update your paperwork. You'll receive an appointment reminder the day before. Anything else I can help with?

**Caller:** No, that's everything. Thank you.

**AI:** You're very welcome, Ms. Park. We'll see you Wednesday. Take care.

## Call 4: Home Services - HVAC Service Request

**AI:** Good afternoon, All Seasons Heating and Cooling. This is Lisa. How can I help you today?

**Caller:** Hi, my air conditioner isn't working and it's getting really hot in the house.

**AI:** I'm sorry to hear that. Let me get some information and see how quickly we can get someone out to you. First, what's your address?

**Caller:** 742 Oak Street.

**AI:** Thank you. And your name please?

**Caller:** David Chen.

**AI:** Thanks Mr. Chen. Can you describe what's happening with the unit? Is it making any unusual sounds, or is it just not turning on?

**Caller:** It's running but not blowing cold air. Just warm air coming out.

**AI:** That could be a refrigerant issue or a compressor problem. We have a technician available this afternoon between 2 and 4 PM, or we could schedule for tomorrow morning. Which works better for you?

**Caller:** This afternoon would be great if possible.

**AI:** Perfect. I'll have our technician, Mike, out between 2 and 4 PM today. Our service call fee is $89, which covers the diagnostic. If you need repairs, Mike will provide a quote before doing any work. What's the best phone number to reach you?

**Caller:** 555-4567.

**AI:** Excellent. You'll receive a text 30 minutes before Mike arrives. Is there anything else I can help with today?

**Caller:** No, that's all. Thank you so much.

**AI:** You're very welcome, Mr. Chen. Stay cool, and we'll see you this afternoon.
