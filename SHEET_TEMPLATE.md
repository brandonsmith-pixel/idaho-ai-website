## Manual Setup

Create a new Google Sheet with these columns in Row 1:

| A | B | C | D | E | F | G | H | I | J | K | L | M | N |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timestamp | Business Name | Business Phone | Test Phone | Industry | Website | Address | Hours | Services | Pricing | Booking Process | FAQs | Additional Info | Files |

## Column Descriptions:

- **A - Timestamp**: Auto-generated submission time
- **B - Business Name**: Company/practice name
- **C - Business Phone**: Their main business number
- **D - Test Phone**: Where to call for demo
- **E - Industry**: restaurant/medical/retail/salon/professional/other
- **F - Website**: Their website URL
- **G - Address**: Physical location
- **H - Hours**: Business hours
- **I - Services**: What they offer
- **J - Pricing**: Price information
- **K - Booking Process**: How to schedule
- **L - FAQs**: Q&A pairs
- **M - Additional Info**: Extra context
- **N - Files**: Uploaded document names

## Formatting Tips:

- **Freeze Row 1** (headers): View → Freeze → 1 row
- **Auto-resize columns**: Select all → Format → Resize columns → Fit to data
- **Add alternating colors**: Format → Alternating colors
- **Sort by timestamp**: Data → Create a filter → Click A1 dropdown → Sort Z→A
