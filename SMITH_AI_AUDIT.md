# Smith.ai vs Teton Group AI Receptionist - UX Audit

## What Smith.ai Does Better (Features We're Missing)

### 1. ✅ HAVE: Sample Call Player
- Smith.ai: Interactive audio player with synchronized transcripts
- Us: ✅ We have this (SampleCallPlayer component)

### 2. ❌ MISSING: Pricing Calculator / ROI Calculator
- Smith.ai: Interactive calculator showing cost savings
- Us: Missing - just flat pricing
- **Impact:** High - helps justify purchase

### 3. ❌ MISSING: Live Chat Widget
- Smith.ai: Instant chat available on every page
- Us: Missing - only contact form
- **Impact:** Medium - reduces friction for questions

### 4. ❌ MISSING: Video Testimonials / Case Study Videos
- Smith.ai: Video testimonials from real customers
- Us: Missing - only text testimonials
- **Impact:** High - video builds more trust

### 5. ✅ HAVE: Real Business Names in Case Studies
- Smith.ai: Shows real company names
- Us: ✅ We use real names (Boise Medical Group, etc.)

### 6. ❌ MISSING: Integration Logos
- Smith.ai: Shows logos of all integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zapier, etc.)
- Us: Missing - we mention Google Calendar but no visual proof
- **Impact:** Medium - builds credibility

### 7. ❌ MISSING: Industry-Specific Landing Pages
- Smith.ai: Separate pages for law firms, medical, etc.
- Us: Generic page only
- **Impact:** High - better conversion for targeted ads

### 8. ❌ MISSING: "How It Works" Visual Flow
- Smith.ai: Step-by-step visual diagram
- Us: We have text but no visual flow
- **Impact:** Medium - easier to understand

### 9. ❌ MISSING: Money-Back Guarantee Badge
- Smith.ai: Prominent "30-day money-back guarantee" badge
- Us: We mention it but not prominently
- **Impact:** Medium - reduces purchase anxiety

### 10. ❌ MISSING: Live Activity Feed
- Smith.ai: "X calls answered today" counter
- Us: Missing
- **Impact:** Low - social proof

### 11. ❌ MISSING: Comparison Table
- Smith.ai: "Us vs Hiring a Receptionist" comparison
- Us: Missing
- **Impact:** High - makes ROI obvious

### 12. ❌ MISSING: Call Transcript Examples in Hero
- Smith.ai: Shows actual transcript snippets above the fold
- Us: Sample calls section lower on page
- **Impact:** Medium - faster proof

### 13. ❌ MISSING: Sticky CTA Bar
- Smith.ai: Fixed bottom bar with CTA on scroll
- Us: Missing
- **Impact:** Medium - improves conversion

### 14. ❌ MISSING: Exit-Intent Popup
- Smith.ai: Special offer on exit
- Us: Missing
- **Impact:** Medium - recovers abandoning visitors
