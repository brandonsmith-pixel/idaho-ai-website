## Pro Tips for Google Ads Sitelinks

1. **Use All 6:** Google shows up to 8 sitelinks on desktop, 4-6 on mobile. Fill all slots.

2. **Track Performance:** Add UTM parameters to measure which sitelinks drive conversions:
   - Example: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=sitelink_demo`

3. **Rotate Seasonally:** 
   - During holidays: "Holiday Hours Covered"
   - Tax season: "Perfect for CPAs"
   - Industry events: "Used by [Industry] Leaders"

4. **A/B Test:** Try different headlines/descriptions and keep the winners.

5. **Match Search Intent:**
   - Informational searches → "How It Works", "Sample Calls"
   - Transactional searches → "Try Free Demo", "Pricing"
