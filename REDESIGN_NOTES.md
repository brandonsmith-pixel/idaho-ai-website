# Landing Page Redesign - smith.ai Style

## Current Issues to Fix:
1. Too much color/gradient (looks "AI-generated")
2. Form is too long and overwhelming
3. No clear trust signals up front
4. Missing social proof
5. Too much text before CTA

## smith.ai Best Practices to Apply:

### 1. Hero Section
**smith.ai does:**
- Clear headline: "24/7 Answering Service"
- Sub-headline: Simple benefit statement
- Single CTA button: "Get Started"
- Trust badge: BBB Accredited, 5-star reviews

**We should do:**
- Headline: "Never Miss Another Call"
- Sub-headline: "AI receptionist answers 24/7. Set up in 5 minutes. Try free demo."
- CTA: "Try Free Demo" (prominent blue button)
- Trust: "Trusted by 50+ businesses • 4.9/5 stars"

### 2. Social Proof Section
**smith.ai does:**
- Customer logos (recognizable brands)
- Review stars and ratings
- Testimonial quotes with photos

**We should do:**
- "As seen in" logos (if applicable)
- Google reviews aggregate (4.9/5 ⭐)
- 1-2 short testimonials with business names

### 3. How It Works (3 Steps)
**smith.ai does:**
- Icon + short title + 1 sentence
- Very simple, scannable
- CTA after the 3 steps

**We should do:**
1. "Try Free Demo" (60 seconds)
2. "Choose Your Plan" ($99 or $500)
3. "Go Live" (AI answers calls)

### 4. Features/Benefits
**smith.ai does:**
- Benefit headlines (not feature headlines)
- "Never miss a lead" not "AI-powered technology"
- Icons for visual hierarchy
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max)

**We should do:**
- "Answer Every Call, Even at 3 AM"
- "Book Appointments Automatically"
- "Forward Complex Calls to You"
- "Pay Only for What You Use"

### 5. Pricing
**smith.ai does:**
- Simple 2-3 plan comparison
- Clear "Most Popular" badge
- Bullet points (not paragraphs)
- CTA button on each plan

**We should do:**
- Keep the 2-plan structure
- Add "Most Popular" badge to self-serve
- Simplify bullet points
- Emphasize 7-day guarantee

### 6. Final CTA
**smith.ai does:**
- Blue banner at bottom
- One last CTA: "Get Started Free"
- Phone number for enterprise sales

**We should do:**
- "Ready to Never Miss a Call Again?"
- "Try Free 5-Minute Demo"
- "Questions? Call us: +1 (866) 497-8716"

## Design System to Implement:

### Colors:
- Primary: #2563EB (blue-600, like smith.ai)
- Secondary: #1E40AF (blue-700, darker)
- Accent: #10B981 (green-500, for success states)
- Background: #FFFFFF (white)
- Gray: #F9FAFB (gray-50, subtle sections)
- Text: #111827 (gray-900, high contrast)

### Typography:
- Headings: font-bold, not font-black (less aggressive)
- Body: text-lg (18px) for readability
- Line height: leading-relaxed (more breathing room)

### Spacing:
- More whitespace between sections
- Padding: py-16 or py-20 (not py-8)
- Max width: max-w-7xl (wide, not cramped)

### Components:
- Buttons: Solid blue, white text, rounded-lg (not rounded-xl)
- Cards: border + shadow-sm (subtle, not shadow-2xl)
- Icons: Outline style (not filled)
